CASE STUDY 2024

CrediGo

A digital lending platform that helps individuals and businesses access flexible loans, fast, secure, and tailored to their needs.

CrediGo is a CBN-licensed lending platform designed to make borrowing simple in Nigeria. I designed the full product end-to-end — from the marketing site that brings users in, to the dashboard that keeps them coming back. The brief: build something a first-time borrower can trust in 30 seconds, and a returning borrower can navigate in their sleep.

Problem

How can we design a lending product that feels as safe and intentional as a real bank, but as quick as sending money on Opay?

Outcome

A full digital lending product — marketing landing page, onboarding flow, KYC, multi-product loan selector (HomeFlex / BusinessLoan / QuickFund / CrediMini / FlexLoan / MilestonePay), and a dashboard with loan tracking, repayment goals, and transaction history. Designed for the kind of borrower who reads the fine print.

ROLE

UI/UX Designer & Project Manager

TEAM

Solo (design + brand)

INDUSTRY

Fintech · Lending

Research

The starting point

I looked at how FairMoney, Carbon, Branch, and Renmoney handle the same flows. The pattern was clear: the apps that win in Nigeria don't try to look like Stripe — they look like trust. Big numbers, clear pricing, no jargon, and one obvious next action on every screen.

Reflection

What I'd carry forward

Building CrediGo end-to-end made one thing concrete: trust in fintech is mostly a typography problem. The features matter, but readability, hierarchy, and a single clear next action carry more weight than any specific flow. I'd take this same restraint into the next financial product I touch.
